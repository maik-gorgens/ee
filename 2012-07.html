tiny owl pillow

so. da hab ich zum ersten mal ein pattern geschrieben. ein kleines eulenkissen war mein zweites häkel-zwischenprojekt. sehr einfach zu machen. und da ich eigentlich nur amerikanische anleitungen kann, hab ichs auch auf englisch geschrieben.
gibt ja übersetzungen. zum beispiel hier.


BODY

make two
use brown yarn
start with magic ring
for each round: start with ch 2, which counts as first dc and finish with sl st in top of ch 2 to join the round

round 1 --- ch 2 (counts as first dc), 11 dc in ring, sl st in top of first ch 2 (=12 dc)
round 2 --- 2 dc in each dc around (=24 dc)
round 3 --- *2 dc in first dc, dc in next dc* repeat 12 times (=36 dc)
round 4 --- *2 dc in first dc, dc in next 2 dc* repeat 12 times (=48 dc)
round 5 --- *2 dc in first dc, dc in next 3 dc* repeat 12 times (=60 dc)
round 6 --- *2 dc in first dc, dc in next 4 dc* repeat 12 times (=72 dc)
round 7 --- *2 dc in first dc, dc in next 5 dc* repeat 12 times (=84 dc)
round 8 --- *2 dc in first dc, dc in next 6 dc* repeat 12 times (=96 dc)
round 9 --- *2 dc in first dc, dc in next 7 dc* repeat 12 times (=108 dc)
round 10 --- *2 dc in first dc, dc in next 8 dc* repeat 12 times (=120 dc)
round 11 --- *2 dc in first dc, dc in next 9 dc* repeat 12 times (=132 dc)

EYE

make two
use white, black and yellow yarn
crochet just like for the body parts, but with changes in color

round 1 --- black (12 dc)
round 2 --- yellow (24 dc)
round 3 --- black (36 dc)
round 4 --- white (48 dc)
round 5 --- white (60 dc)

BEAK

make one
use yellow yarn

start with a chain of 10. from now only crochet in front loops (top half of the chain)
treble in 4th chain from hook
treble in next two chains
double crochet in next two chains
single crochet in next chain, slip stitch in next chain
(= tr, tr, tr, dc, dc, sc, slip st)
now work your way back on the other side of the chain, only crochet in back loops (other half of the chain)
single crochet in first chain
double crochet in next two chains
treble in next 3 chains
chain 3 and slip stitch in middle of the beak
(= sc, dc, dc, tr, tr, tr, ch3, slip st)

sew beak to middle of one body part, afterwards sew on eyes. sew or crochet on back part of body and stuff
